OUR STORY & HERITAGE

A 25+ Year Morning Landmark in Habsiguda

Located prominently on Street Number 8 Main Road, Sree Swathi Tiffins (Veg) has been the go-to breakfast and meal spot for generations of students, office goers, travellers, and local families.

Operating on a fast, efficient Self-Service (Darshini style) token counter model, we serve fresh tiffins right off the hot tawas from 6:00 AM in the morning to 11:00 PM at night.

Recently Renovated

Cleaner dining space, modernized wash areas, and hygienic food prep.

Extensive Variety

Over 90+ items spanning Dosas, Idlis, Pooris, Meals, Chai & Herbal drinks.

Affordable Everyday Prices

Delicious food at honest prices, making quality tiffins accessible to all.
